You are feeling anxious right now

Anxiety is your body's natural response to stress, often manifesting as worry, restlessness, or physical tension. It's common and manageable with the right tools.

Your mind is racing, but you're not alone. Try these personalized tips:

Breathe in for 4, hold for 4, out for 4 (Box breathing)
Do a quick body scan — notice tension areas
Journal what you're anxious about — name it
Do one tiny thing you have control over
Limit caffeine for now
Play calm background sounds or music
Ground yourself: Name 5 things you can see
Practice progressive muscle relaxation
"Anxiety does not empty tomorrow of its sorrows, but only empties today of its strength." — Charles Spurgeon
