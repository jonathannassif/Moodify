You are feeling stressed right now

Stress is your body's response to pressure or demands. While some stress can be motivating, chronic stress can be harmful. Learning to manage it is key to well-being.

Breathe. You're doing your best. Here are some effective ways to manage stress:

Take a 5-minute walk in fresh air
Do 2 minutes of deep breathing
Write a list of what's overwhelming you
Put your phone on silent for 20 minutes
Listen to a calm instrumental track
Drink a glass of cold water slowly
Try progressive muscle relaxation
Break tasks into smaller, manageable steps
"The greatest weapon against stress is our ability to choose one thought over another." — William James
