You are feeling lost right now

Feeling lost often means you're in a period of transition or uncertainty. It's a normal part of life and can lead to growth and new directions.

It's okay. Everyone feels lost sometimes. Here are some ideas to help you navigate:

Take a deep breath. It's just a chapter, not the whole book.
Write down what you know for sure — anchor yourself.
Talk to someone you trust. Don't isolate.
Try a walk without your phone. Let your mind breathe.
Set small, achievable goals for today.
Explore a new interest or hobby.
Practice self-compassion — be kind to yourself.
Consider seeking guidance from a mentor or counselor.
"Not all those who wander are lost." — J.R.R. Tolkien
