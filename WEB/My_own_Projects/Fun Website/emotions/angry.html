You are feeling angry right now

Anger is a natural emotion. Let’s process it constructively.

Take 10 deep breaths. Slow and steady.
Leave the room or situation if needed.
Scribble or write down how you feel.
Listen to calming or instrumental music.
Move: do some stretches or take a short walk.
Say: “I can choose how I respond.”
“For every minute you remain angry, you give up sixty seconds of peace of mind.” — Ralph Waldo Emerson
